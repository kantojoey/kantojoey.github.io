My name is Alexis Tinoco and I am a Southern California native, though I have also spent time living in St. Louis, MO and Washington, D.C. My professional journey started with my time at Washington University in St. Louis where I graduated with a B.A. in Environmental Biology. During my time as an undergraduate student, I also completed introductory and advanced GIS coursework. After graduating, I pivoted away from environmental science and pursued my interest in spatial data analysis, beginning a role as a GIS Analyst working with jurisdictional tax rate data at Taxography in St. Louis.

In my current role, I produce highly detailed tax rate maps for customers so they can make strategic decisions about their company asset placements. My role requires strong attention to detail, a high level of accuracy, effective communication with the research team to identify and resolve potential blockers, and the ability to handle multiple concurrent projects while prioritizing assignments based around revolving deadlines.

After five years as a spatial data analyst, I am now making the transition into the software development space because I want a role that allows me to explore my creativity to solve complex problems while also offering me more opportunities for growth. Over the last year, I have strengthened my abilities as a programmer, building a strong foundation as a full-stack developer through the LaunchCode software development program. I have loved watching my code grow from just a few lines into a full-fledged project that people can interact with while also appreciating all the improvements I can continue to make across my various projects to provide better user experiences. Now I am looking to apply the skills I have developed over the last five years as a GIS analyst and my abilities as a software engineer to a junior developer role.

I am currently looking for work opportunities as a junior front end or full stack developer. If you would like to talk about a potential position or learn more about me, please feel free to view my resume here or contact me here!
